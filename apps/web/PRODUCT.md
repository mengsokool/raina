# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Raina is used by system owners and operations teams managing IoT devices, telemetry, dashboards, and automations. They often arrive at the admin sign-in screen to inspect or operate a live physical environment, so the path into the product must be calm, clear, and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

Raina is a self-hosted IoT operations platform for configuring projects, monitoring device telemetry, and controlling dashboards and automations. Success means operators can authenticate, understand the system state, and proceed to their work without ambiguity or friction.

## Brand Personality

Joyful, precise, grounded. The interface should feel optimistic and alive without losing the restraint and legibility expected of an operational tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic high-saturation SaaS decoration, empty-white forms that feel detached from the product, and dark cyberpunk control-room styling. Do not trade clarity for visual spectacle.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next operational action obvious.
2. Pair a composed, technical foundation with a small amount of optimistic energy.
3. Let live-system cues support confidence, never compete with the task.
4. Preserve familiar form and dashboard conventions so the product disappears into the workflow.
5. Treat accessibility, errors, and recovery as primary interface states.
6. Keep the interface quiet: remove instructions, descriptions, and labels unless they help a user decide, act, or recover from an error.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast expectations, keep all authentication actions keyboard-accessible, provide explicit labels and error feedback, retain visible focus styles, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
